# Soulstone Survivors development and demo marketing postmortem

Checked 2026-10-08. Published article: `_posts/2026-10-08-soulstone-survivors-demo-marketing-postmortem.md`.

## Data and reproduction

The public data file is `files/soulstone-survivors-postmortem/data.json`. It stores dates, units, source URLs, and interpretation notes. Run `python render_figures.py` with matplotlib installed to regenerate the two original charts. Set `SOULSTONE_FONT` to a local CJK font if needed. The script uses no private analytics.

- SteamDB CCU peaks belong to three separate applications and dates; do not sum them or derive conversion rates.
- The 2022-08-04 HTMAG report states a month of 34,724 wishlist additions but does not identify the period. The demo release was July 27 UTC, announced July 28. The article does not treat that figure as a full month after the demo or an October festival result.
- The October survey reports 41 surveyed games but does not separately disclose the playtime question's response count. Eighteen minutes is the median of game-level median playtimes, not a pooled player median. Three hours is the Soulstone demo's median playtime, not session duration.
- The festival screenshot is a historical Popular Upcoming tab state. It is not a final rank or a labeled wishlist dataset.
- The original three characters were replaced by three different characters in the August prologue. All six were combined in September's demo.
- The six-character demo's October 12 removal was the announced 2022 plan, not a claim that no demo ever returned later.
- The China collaboration began in August 2023. Partner-reported sales shares and Heybox wishlists are kept distinct from 2022 Steam demo metrics. The 12.9% to 20% change equals 7.1 percentage points.
- The 1.3 million sales disclosure is all-platform cumulative sales as of September 3, 2026, not a 2022 launch or Steam-only figure.
